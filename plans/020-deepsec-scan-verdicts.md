## What is genuinely left

- **`lib/lock.ts` stale-lock race** — accepted. Documented as the trade-off the design chose;
  changing it means changing how concurrent `agy` runs are admitted, which is its own session.
- **Cross-process config updates are atomic but not serialized.** Two writers can each read,
  merge and rename, and one patch disappears. Nothing corrupts; one setting can be lost.
- **`plan` and `sandbox` spend tokens without a dialog.** That is what those modes are for.
  34dbac9 added the dialog for content leaving the machine, which is the part that cannot be
  taken back; token spend is visible in the run itself.
